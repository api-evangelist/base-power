# Base Power Company

> Base Power is a home energy company serving Texas and Illinois that pairs a home battery with retail electricity service. The battery keeps a home powered during outages and helps lower the energy bill, and Base can be the electricity provider with or without a battery.

Base Texas REP, LLC — PUCT License #10338. Base is a registered Retail Electric Provider with the Public Utility Commission of Texas and also operates in Illinois.

Key facts:
- What it is: a home battery installed at the home, plus monthly energy service and installation, offered together. Solar is not required, and the battery also works with existing solar.
- How it stays affordable: Base batteries charge when grid demand is low and send power back to the grid when demand is high (grid-balancing). Revenue from grid-balancing helps keep member energy rates low.
- Backup: during an outage the battery powers the home automatically, with no manual switch. Home backup is a separate product from electricity service — it backs up the home and does not change how the local utility delivers power.
- Texas service areas: the power-to-choose areas served by Oncor, CenterPoint, AEP Texas, and TNMP, plus additional utility and cooperative areas including CoServ, GVEC, Farmers Electric, and El Paso Electric. Illinois: ComEd.
- Leadership: Zach Dell (chief executive officer) and co-founder Justin Lopas. Headquarters: Austin, Texas.
- Recognition: named to the TIME100 Most Influential Companies (2026); investors include Andreessen Horowitz and Lightspeed Venture Partners.
- Pricing varies by location and the service chosen. See the pricing pages below for current details; do not infer a specific rate or fee from this file.
